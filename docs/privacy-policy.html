Patristica

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Short version: Patristica has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not build any record of what you read. Your notes, highlights and history are kept on your device. Some features load content from the internet, and the services they contact can see your IP address, as with any website.

Overview

Patristica ("the app") is a free Bible study application developed by Solendor. This policy explains what stays on your device, what is sent over the internet, and who receives it.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or accounts
  • No usage analytics or statistics
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers

We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

Stored on Your Device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device. We never receive it:

  • Bookmarks, notes and highlights
  • Reading history, search history and reading plans
  • Your settings, including reminder times
  • Your own ESV API key, if you choose to add one
  • Any content packs you download

It is removed from the device when you uninstall the app. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Google may include app data in your own Google backup; that is controlled by your device settings. Daily reminders are scheduled on the device itself.

Internet Access

The King James Version and much of the study material work offline. The features below use the internet. Whoever receives a request can see your IP address and what was requested, and handles it under its own privacy policy:

  • Online texts, commentary, lexicons, release notes and pack downloads are loaded from GitHub.
  • App updates are checked for and delivered by Expo, which receives basic technical details such as your platform and app version and a random installation ID. Google Play also delivers updates.
  • Maps load map images from CARTO and map software from unpkg.
  • The ESV Bible is loaded as described in the next section.
  • Links you tap, such as "Read in full", open the website you chose.

The ESV Bible

The ESV is provided by Crossway and is only contacted if you choose to read it.

  • Without your own key: the app asks our server, hosted on Cloudflare, for the chapter. Our server requests it from Crossway using our key and passes it back. Crossway sees our server's request, not yours. To prevent abuse, the server counts requests by IP address. Those counts are held in temporary memory, reset daily, and are not written to storage. We keep no log of which chapters are requested, and the server stores no ESV text. Cloudflare processes the connection as our hosting provider.
  • With your own key: the app sends your key and the chapter request directly to Crossway, and our server is not involved.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no advertising networks, analytics services or social logins. The services it may contact are GitHub, Expo, CARTO, unpkg, Cloudflare, Crossway and Google Play, for the purposes described above.

Children's Privacy

The app does not ask anyone for personal information and has no accounts, messaging or advertising. The app contains no content harmful to minors.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Any future version that collects personal data will require explicit notice and consent.
