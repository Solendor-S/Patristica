Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Short version: Patristica collects no data whatsoever. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Overview

Patristica ("the app") is a Bible study application developed by Solendor. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices. In short: we do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or usage data.

Data Collection

We collect no data of any kind. This includes:

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • No usage analytics or statistics
  • No crash reports or diagnostic data
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No advertising identifiers

Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Bookmarks you create
  • Notes you write on verses
  • Highlights you apply to verses
  • Your reading history
  • Your selected Bible translation preference

This data never leaves your device. It is stored in a local SQLite database and is only accessible to the app. It is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Internet Access

Patristica functions entirely offline. The app does not make any network requests during normal use. All Bible text, Church Father commentary, Greek and Hebrew word data, and historical sources are bundled with the app.

The only exception is app updates delivered through the Google Play Store, which are handled entirely by Google and not by us.

Third-Party Services

Patristica does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social login providers. There are no third parties receiving any data through this app.

Children's Privacy

Because we collect no data from anyone, there are no special concerns regarding children's privacy. The app contains no content harmful to minors.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Any future version that collects data will require explicit notice and consent.
